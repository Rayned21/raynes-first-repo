## Brand & Style

This design system channels the electric wonder, joyful anticipation, and sensory delight of entering the Big Top. Built specifically for children and families, the aesthetic blends vintage circus showmanship with playful modern digital design. It prioritizes discovery, celebration, tactile joy, and clear visual signposts.

The visual style is **Tactile Neo-Carnival**: rich dimensional layers, bouncy physical metaphors, starburst and pennant motifs, marquee framing, and ticket-stub containers. Every interaction feels alive, kinetic, and celebratory without overwhelming young users with unguided clutter. High color contrast and robust silhouettes guarantee legibility and ease of use across touchscreens.

## Colors

The palette captures the spectacle of sunlit carnival grounds and midnight Big Top performances:

- **Primary (Big Top Crimson - `#D90429` / `#E63946`):** The commanding ringmaster red used for primary calls to action, high-energy banners, marquee highlights, and celebratory states.
- **Secondary (Popcorn Gold / Circus Glow - `#FFB703` / `#FFCA3A`):** Radiating warmth, curiosity, and festive energy; perfect for badges, stars, illumination accents, and high-priority secondary prompts.
- **Tertiary (Midway Teal & Candy Pop - `#06D6A0` / `#118AB2`):** Crisp contrasts evoking acrobat silks, aerial ribbons, and sugary spun-cotton candy treats. Used for positive progress, interactive toggles, and playful counter-accents.
- **Neutral (Midnight Tent Navy - `#1D203B` / Deep Stage `#0F1123`):** Replaces sterile grays with the deep, dramatic atmospheric shade of nightfall outside the tent. Provides grounded contrast for typography, thick borders, and framing.
- **Surface / Background (Marquee Ivory - `#FFFDF7` & Popcorn Creme `#FFF7E6`):** Soft, warm foundational backgrounds that eliminate harsh digital white glare while maintaining optimal contrast.

## Typography

The type system blends theatrical impact with high kid-friendly readability. 

- **Headline Font (`Bricolage Grotesque`):** Delivers whimsical curves, exuberant punchy letterforms, and carnival-poster confidence without losing structural clarity. Display styles feature slight upward bounce and tight tracking.
- **Body & Label Font (`Rubik`):** A geometric, soft-cornered sans-serif that ensures effortless comprehension for early readers. Its gentle rounded strokes echo the soft balloon and circus canopy shapes throughout the UI.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Set in bold weights of `Rubik` with slight positive tracking, imitating vintage circus entrance badges, prize cards, and fairground ticket stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a versatile fluid grid system calibrated for dynamic, finger-friendly touch targets and energetic composition:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Mobile (<600px):** 4-column layout, 16px gutters, 20px outer margin.
  - **Tablet (600px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, 24px gutters, 32px outer margin.
  - **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout, 32px gutters, 48px outer margin, max content width capped at 1280px.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Elements follow an 8px base rhythm with generous vertical clearance around interactive focal points. Sections can break rigid baseline alignments with gentle tilts (-2° to +2° rosettes and cards) to convey spontaneous carnival movement.
- **Touch Targets:** Interactive areas maintain a mandatory minimum target size of 48×48px, expanding to 56×56px for primary child navigation triggers.

## Elevation & Depth

This system achieves visual hierarchy using **Tactile Toybox Depth**—firm solid drop shadows combined with crisp borders instead of realistic blurred shadows:

- **Level 0 (Stage Ground):** Flat parchment or navy backdrop with subtle carnival stripe overlays (10% tint).
- **Level 1 (Booths & Panels):** 2.5px solid border in Midnight Tent Navy (`#1D203B`) paired with an offset hard shadow of `0px 4px 0px #1D203B`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Tickets):** 3px solid border in Midnight Tent Navy with an offset hard shadow of `0px 6px 0px #1D203B`. On hover/touch, the element translates down 2px with the shadow shrinking to `0px 4px 0px`.
- **Level 3 (Floating Modals & Grand Marquees):** 4px solid border with an offset shadow of `0px 10px 0px #0F1123`, often adorned with flashing marquee dot accents (repeating gold/cream studs).
- **Pressed State:** Elements translate down 4px with `0px 1px 0px #1D203B` shadow, providing instant tactile feedback like an arcade or midway button.

## Shapes

The shape language reflects fairground balloons, ticket stubs, and circus props:

- **Primary Geometry:** Base radius of `1rem` (16px) with large containers flexing to `1.5rem` (24px) for safe, friendly curves.
- **Ticket Stubs & Badges:** Scalloped corners, inward circular notches (radius cutouts), and perforated dashed division lines.
- **Marquee Badges:** Pill shapes and 8-point carnival stars framing counters, rank levels, and celebration badges.
- **Flags & Ribbons:** Triangular pennant bunting and scalloped borders applied along top navigations and card headers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Grand Ringmaster"):** Popcorn Gold (`#FFB703`) background with Crimson (`#D90429`) borders (3px) and hard navy drop shadow (`0 4px 0 #1D203B`). Bold uppercase typography with left/right circus star sparkles (`★`).
- **Secondary ("Acrobat Sky"):** Teal (`#06D6A0`) fill, solid dark outline, crisp white text.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Cream background with dashed crimson marquee border.

### Ticket Cards
- Styled like vintage admission passes. Features semi-circle cutouts on opposing edges, a dashed perforation divider line, and contrasting header bands with gold star icons. Includes Level 2 tactile hard shadows.

### Chips & Badges
- Fully rounded pills with thick 2px borders. Category chips use pastel variations (cotton candy teal, lemon frost yellow, circus bubble pink) paired with bold dark navy labels and small carnival icon prefixes.

### Inputs & Selectors
- Chunky 52px minimum height with 2.5px solid outlines and Marquee Ivory background. Focus states ignite a warm glow ring of Popcorn Gold (`#FFCA3A`) with an inner Midnight Navy stroke. Helper labels feature bold, friendly prompts.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Oversized (28×28px) tactile controls. Checked states snap in with a punchy gold circus star icon (`★`) or bold midway bullseye circle against a vibrant crimson fill.

### Marquee Headers & Progress Bars
- Headers feature alternating illuminated light bulb borders (golden yellow and cream circular dots).
- Progress trackers mimic carnival high-striker bell towers ("Ring the Bell!"), filling upward with animated candy-stripe gradient segments.
